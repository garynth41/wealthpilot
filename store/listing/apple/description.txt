WealthPilot puts your household's money in one place: what's coming in, what's going out, what's due, and the plan for what comes next. Share it with your partner or family, each with exactly the access you choose.

EVERYDAY MONEY
• Budgets by month, paycheck, week, every two weeks or twice a month, with rollover and a clear amount left to spend
• Safe to spend: what you can spend today after upcoming bills, overdue bills and your cash floor
• Bills and subscriptions with due dates, autopay, reminders on your phone and one-tap Mark paid
• Give every paycheck a job the moment it lands
• Transactions with categories, splits, tags, comments, receipts and payee rules that file things for you
• Import bank files (CSV, OFX, QFX), or connect your banks through SimpleFIN Bridge for a daily sync
• Reconcile accounts against your statements, checkbook style

DEBTS AND GOALS
• Debt payoff plans, avalanche or snowball, that know when a 0% promotion ends
• Savings goals with target dates, milestones and contribution history
• Credit scores over time

THE BIGGER PICTURE
• Net worth across accounts, property and vehicles
• A cash-flow forecast with what-if changes
• Investments with tax lots, gains and allocation
• Retirement projections, Social Security timing and a Monte Carlo check
• Income-tax estimates: federal, Ohio in full, and a simple rate for other states
• Insurance, an estate checklist, and a hardship plan for tight months
• Reports and trends you can export to Excel or CSV

MADE FOR A HOUSEHOLD
• Invite people as owners, editors or viewers, or limit someone to the sections they need
• See who changed what, and when
• Opens without a connection with a read-only copy, and catches up when you're back online

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads, no tracking, and your data is never sold
• Face ID or Touch ID unlock, two-factor sign-in, and a one-tap switch to hide amounts
• Export everything at any time, and delete your account right in the app
