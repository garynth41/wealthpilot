Delete your WealthPilot account

Last updated November 7, 2026

You can delete your WealthPilot account and its information yourself, at any time, in the app or on the website — no need to install anything or contact anyone.

Delete it yourself

  1. Open the WealthPilot app, or go to the WealthPilot website on any computer or phone, and sign in.
  2. Open Settings → Security and scroll to Delete my account. (Signed in but no household yet? Choose Delete my account on the welcome screen.)
  3. Choose Delete my account…. The app shows exactly what will be deleted and what you will leave before anything happens. If you might want a copy, choose Save a backup first.
  4. Type your password and the words delete my account, then choose Delete my account permanently.

Open WealthPilot to delete your account

If two-factor sign-in is on, sign in again with your code just before deleting. If you are the only owner of a household that other people use, make one of them an owner (Settings → Members) or delete that household first — the app tells you if this applies.

What is deleted, and what is kept

InformationWhat happens
Your sign-in: email address, password, two-factor and passkeysDeleted immediately.
Households where you are the only member — every account, transaction, budget, bill, goal, note, attached receipt or statement, bank connection and nightly backupDeleted immediately.
Households you share with other peopleKept for the other members, with any files you added. You are removed, your alert choices go, and wherever your name or email address appears in their history it becomes “a former member”. Their nightly backups made before that keep your earlier details until those backups are deleted, within 30 days.
Invitations sent to your email addressDeleted.
Error reportsYour user ID is removed from them; the reports are deleted after 90 days.
A record that a deleted household existed (its name, when it was deleted and how many files were removed — no financial information)Kept, so questions about the deletion can be answered.
Routine security logs at our hosting providers (for example sign-in times and IP addresses)Expire under the providers’ own retention periods.
Copies on your own devices — the offline copy and any backup files you savedThe offline copy is removed when you sign out; delete saved backup files yourself.

Deleting an account cannot be undone. Bank information held by SimpleFIN Bridge is under your SimpleFIN account; delete it there as well if you no longer want it.

Delete some information but keep your account

  • A whole household (owners): Settings → General → Delete this household. It stays read-only for 7 days, so any owner can change their mind, and is then removed for good with its files and backups.
  • Anything else: delete individual transactions, accounts, bills, attachments and other records where you see them in the app.
  • The bank connection: Connections → Automatic bank sync → Disconnect. The saved connection key is deleted at once.
  • Email capture (website): clear the Gmail or Outlook setup on the Email page. Mailbox sign-ins are never stored on our servers.

Can’t sign in?

Choose Forgot password? on the sign-in screen to set a new password, then follow the steps above. If you still cannot get in, email the support address shown on the WealthPilot listing in the App Store or Google Play from the email address on your account, and ask for the account to be deleted. Once it is confirmed the account is yours, it is deleted as described above within 30 days, and you get a reply when it is done.
