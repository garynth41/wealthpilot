Support

Cresari keeps a household’s budget, bills, accounts and plans in one place — on the website and in the iPhone and Android apps, all with the same account.

Help is built into the app. Every page has a Help button (ⓘ) that explains that page, each card has a ? next to its title, and Help Center in the menu lists every page, a glossary and step-by-step answers.

Common questions

Can I try it without an account?

Yes. On the sign-in screen, choose Open the offline demo. It is filled with sample data, stays on your device and can be reset at any time.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, choose Forgot password? and enter your email address. The link in the email opens the Cresari website, where you choose a new password; then sign in with it on the website or in the app.

I lost the phone with my two-factor codes

After your password, choose Lost your phone? Use a recovery code and enter one of the recovery codes you saved when you turned on two-factor sign-in. Then set up two-factor again under Settings → Security.

I did not get the confirmation or invitation email

Check your spam or junk folder, and that the address is spelled correctly. An invitation to a household needs no special link: sign in — or create your account — with the invited email address and the household appears.

How do I connect my bank?

Open Connections and follow Automatic bank sync (SimpleFIN). You need your own SimpleFIN Bridge account, where you connect your banks; you then paste its setup token into Cresari. Accounts, balances and transactions arrive every day after that, and you can always import a statement file instead.

How do bill reminders work on my phone?

In the app, open Settings → Notifications and turn on Reminders on this phone, then allow notifications when the phone asks. If you said no before, allow notifications for Cresari in the phone’s own Settings and turn the switch on again. Reminders come a few days before each bill (set per bill on the Bills page) and on the due day.

Can I unlock with Face ID or my fingerprint?

Yes. In the iPhone and Android apps, when the idle lock appears after a period without use, the app offers Face ID, Touch ID or your fingerprint, with your password as the alternative.

How do I export my information?

Settings → Backup & data: download a complete backup file (which can be restored later) or your transactions as a spreadsheet. In the apps, the share sheet opens so you can save the file to Files or Drive, or send it.

How do I share the household with someone?

An owner opens Settings → Members → Invite someone, enters their email address and chooses what they can see and change. Access can be changed or removed at any time.

How do I delete my account or my information?

See Delete your account. You can also delete a single household, or any individual record, without deleting your account.

Is my information safe?

Your information is encrypted, the database returns it only to members of your household, and there are no ads or tracking. The privacy policy has the details.

How do I get the latest version?

The website updates itself. The apps update from the App Store or Google Play; if an important update is needed, the app tells you.

Contact

  • Email: use the support address shown on the Cresari listing in the App Store or Google Play. Please email — never post publicly — anything about your account, your information or a security problem.
  • Problems and suggestions: open an issue at github.com/garynth41/wealthpilot/issues. Issues are public, so leave out names, amounts and anything else personal.

When you write, say which device you use (website, iPhone or Android) and the version shown at the bottom of the sign-in screen or under Settings → General.
